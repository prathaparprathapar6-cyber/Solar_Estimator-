## 3. Architecture (UI)
- **Entry:** `#nameGate` login / admin gate
- **Shell:** `.sidebar` + `.main` + **`.ribbon-wrap`** (replaces classic topbar)
- **Views:** `switchView('dashboard'|'studio'|'bom'|'financials'|…)`
- **Admin:** `openAdminManualModal()` → tabs via `switchAdminTab(...)`
- **State:** `sites[]`, `activeSiteIndex`, `current`, `localStorage` keys prefixed `voltroof_`
- **Feature flags:** `window.VR_FLAGS` · project meta `window.VR_PROJECT` (schema v:2)
